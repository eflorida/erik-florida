# Personal Site — Product Summary

The personal site is a high-signal, evidence-rich career website for engineering-leadership opportunities. It is not a conventional online résumé and must not become an open-ended portfolio project.

## Outcome

A hiring manager should quickly understand:

1. who Erik is as an engineering leader;
2. what evidence supports the positioning;
3. why his experience is relevant to organizations adapting to AI and agentic software development.

## Positioning

Engineering leader and hands-on technical architect focused on building high-performing teams, modern software systems, and AI-native engineering workflows.

The differentiation is the combination of first-version product ownership, technical and organizational judgment, and practical participation in agentic software development. Present a leader who can shape both the product and the engineering organization without moving away from technical work. Mature-engineering experience informs when structure is useful and when it is premature: earn complexity.

## Editorial direction — M001 and M002 review

Lead with who Erik is, the work he has done, and his technical and leadership perspective. The homepage should present experience and evidence of judgment prominently, with Mission Control and delivery process supporting that professional story. Hiring interest should follow naturally from the work and ideas on display; avoid appeals to be hired, urgency, or language that sounds like a job-search plea.

Keep the voice matter-of-fact, precise, and confident. Use personal context where it establishes experience or authorship; avoid an overly familiar, chatty, or promotional voice. The first article, “Verification Over Understanding,” is the accepted tone reference for future drafting.

The homepage should read as a connected professional journey, not a list of accomplishments or a directory of Experience anchors. Lead the narrative with the current focus on agentic development, then ground that direction in hands-on building, team ownership, and engineering judgment. Give each idea a distinct section with short paragraphs rather than packing the history into a long block. Feature the patent early as a compact supporting credential with clear co-inventor attribution; it is one piece of evidence, not the central claim of the page. Detailed project accounts stay on Experience.

The additional homepage positioning guidance prioritizes agentic engineering as a change to the whole engineering model, not an AI-tool skill category. Connect personal experience to clear principles: autonomous engineers, useful feedback, verification, customer outcomes, and structure that solves an observed constraint. Do not frame the story as leaving engineering for management. Signal audience fit through this point of view rather than explicit desired titles, company stages, or headcounts. Distinguish personal AI practice from the developing Mission Control methodology and avoid claims of an already completed organizational transformation.

M002 uses Erik's supplied career master record to implement an experience-led homepage and an Experience page with career progression, selected work, and public patent evidence. The record was assessed for source accuracy and public suitability; selected facts live in validated career data, while provenance and exclusions live in `docs/reference/career-source-assessment.md`. The raw source is not repository or application content. Erik accepted the revised wording and presentation for the M002 commit after local review.

## Product rules

- Evidence over claims.
- Progressive disclosure from a 30-second scan to substantial technical depth.
- Public-safe content and accurate attribution by design.
- Opinionated defaults with explicit reasons to choose differently.
- The implementation itself is evidence of engineering quality.
- Shipping the job-search surface takes priority over completeness.
- Agentic Systems Lab is complementary evidence, not a prerequisite for the website launch.

# Personal Site — Product Summary

The personal site is a high-signal, evidence-rich career website for engineering-leadership opportunities. It is not a conventional online résumé and must not become an open-ended portfolio project.

## Outcome

A hiring manager should quickly understand:

1. who Erik is as an engineering leader;
2. what evidence supports the positioning;
3. why his experience is relevant to organizations adapting to AI and agentic software development.

## Positioning

Engineering leader and hands-on technical architect focused on building high-performing teams, modern software systems, and AI-native engineering workflows.

## Product rules

- Evidence over claims.
- Progressive disclosure from a 30-second scan to substantial technical depth.
- Public-safe content and accurate attribution by design.
- Opinionated defaults with explicit reasons to choose differently.
- The implementation itself is evidence of engineering quality.
- Shipping the job-search surface takes priority over completeness.
- Agentic Systems Lab is complementary evidence, not a prerequisite for the website launch.

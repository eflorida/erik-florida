# Flo Forge

## Purpose and audience

Flo Forge is Erik Florida's umbrella company for his SaaS products. This independent company website introduces the brand and its AI-first engineering ethos to prospective product users, collaborators, and partners. It is not a client-services agency, a product dashboard, or the personal portfolio.

## Authorized direction

Erik's 2026-10-31 brief authorizes a new single-page app, original brand/ethos copy, reasonable design assumptions, dark-but-not-black styling, concrete texture, coordinated transitions, and forge-like sparks. The supplied orange/gray SVGs anchor the identity. Erik accepted the initial visual/copy direction on 2026-10-31, with the requested slogan “AI is the engine, People are the driver.” and email-only contact.

The core promise is useful SaaS shaped around real work: AI as part of how products are engineered and how they can create value, paired with human judgment and durable software fundamentals. The forge metaphor represents purposeful transformation and craft. Visual references are concrete, machined steel, heat, and sparks rather than a literal blacksmith business.

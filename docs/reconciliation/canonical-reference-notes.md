# Canonical reference scope and provenance

**Established:** 2026-09-25, Step 2a of the [reconciliation audit](mission-control-sync-audit.md).
**Authority:** Erik's approval to execute Step 2a and his clarification of the Mission Control / Flight Deck relationship on that date.

## Governing clarification

Mission Control is the methodology. It is generally tool- and team-agnostic, and its principles apply to work in this repository regardless of the tools or application being built.

Flight Deck is a planned, opinionated implementation of a Mission Control engineering team. A key benefit is an observability layer connecting intent, work, state, evidence and decisions; its intended value also includes execution coordination and governance. In principle, a combination of existing tools and operating practices could replace every Flight Deck capability while satisfying the same methodology. Flight Deck is optional, including for sustained operation, rather than a required destination after initial adoption.

Flight Deck-specific ownership, interfaces, adapters, storage and deployment choices apply only when designing the actual Flight Deck application. They are not requirements for the Erik Florida site, the Lab, or Mission Control generally. Existing Flight Deck plans are product design inputs that must be reassessed against the latest Mission Control principles before implementation. Where they conflict, current methodology and explicit human decisions govern; the product plan does not redefine the methodology.

This clarification resolves the audit's broad ownership/dependency question. It does not choose a system of record, technical topology, final demo, or replacement tool combination.

## Definitions and decisions still open

- The package does not define the exact **Flight Deck Runtime / Control Plane / Experience** decomposition. These are future product-design terms; their absence does not block applying Mission Control here. No separate services, applications, or packages are implied.
- **Mission Log** is named and history/provenance responsibilities are described, but a precise event schema, projection, storage and retention contract is not supplied. Preserve attributable history now; design normalized events when an approved implementation requires them.
- **Deployment Topology** must locate authority, storage, changes, observation, access and permissions for a selected implementation. No authority location or adapter design is chosen by this clarification. Flight Deck-specific topology applies only if that product is selected.
- The full Agentic Architecture Reference and To the Moon documents referenced by suite alignment are absent from this package. Their contents and publication status cannot be inferred.
- Framework, harness integration, analytics infrastructure, and final demo selection remain later decisions. Public wording and publication approval remain separate from internal concept alignment.

These open items are scoped design work, not grounds to require Flight Deck or postpone every useful Mission Control practice.

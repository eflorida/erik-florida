## Scope and method

This was a fresh, read-only review of the reconciliation inputs and repository facts. The only authored output is this evaluation. No canonical source, governing document, application source, fixture, test, configuration, publication state, deployment, mission status, or unresolved product decision was changed.

The review covered:

- the [canonical reference index](../mission-control-reference/README.md), all six imported reference documents, and the [source authority/provenance notes](canonical-reference-notes.md);
- the [reconciliation plan](../RECONCILIATION_PLAN.md), [audit and approval record](mission-control-sync-audit.md), root and application agent entry points, product summaries, architecture, ADR-004, implementation options, plans, missions, guides, and source/evidence assessments relevant to Mission Control, Flight Deck, the site, and Agentic Systems Lab;
- current visitor-facing source for the site overview, article, career copy, Lab replay, and live review;
- repository structure, package/workspace/CI/deployment configuration, Git history and refs, content contracts, provider boundary, and focused tests needed to distinguish schema/rendering checks from execution or provider evidence.

The evaluation compared current claims with observed source and repository state. Historical mission and plan records were treated as checkpoint evidence, not current instructions, unless a current governing document adopted them. Public source, editorial publication approval, indexing, and current hosted availability were assessed separately. No hosted site or Lab instance, provider request, deployment control plane, external fixture repository, CI artifact store, or separate Flight Deck checkout was inspected.

Results use these meanings:

- **Pass:** the criterion is satisfied without a material contradiction.
- **Qualified pass:** the governing model is sound, with a bounded, disclosed limitation that does not reverse the result.
- **Fail:** a material contradiction can misstate evidence, authority, current phase, or adopted architecture.

## Contradictions by materiality

### Material

1. **Replay verification claims exceed retained provenance.** The fixture and renderer assert recorded, passed, and verified execution facts, while the governing assessment says those facts are authored assertions without attributable execution evidence in this checkout. This can mislead a visitor about what was actually run and verified. It blocks Human Gate 3 unless the authorized human supplies provenance, approves an illustrative disclosure, replaces the fixture, or explicitly accepts a narrower resolution through Step 2.
2. **The named agent handoff conflicts with the authorized phase.** The retained handoff orders Step 1 and prohibits Step 2, whereas current state and the audit record Human Gate 2 approval and Step 3 authorization. Because the audit cites the handoff as authority, this is more than harmless historical prose: it can direct a coding harness to the wrong work and gate.

### Non-material but actionable

1. **Draft site terminology trails the reconciled model.** “Future engineering harness” can imply a coding-harness replacement or inevitable product, and the two autonomy sentences can imply expansion as the expected destination. Nearby copy and internal guidance limit those readings, both items are drafts, and no current hosting was verified. Resolve the wording during the already-pending editorial decision before publication.
2. **The current-state Git relation is stale.** The remote URL is correct, but local `main` was not ahead of the local `origin/main` ref during evaluation. Branch divergence is volatile and should either be refreshed when stated or expressed as dated evidence.
3. **The imported package has known navigation/completeness gaps.** Four references point to the absent filename `07_Mission_Control_Implementation.md`, and two named suite documents are absent. The provenance notes provide the correct local implementation link and keep the missing definitions open, so these do not currently cause a concept error.

## Overall determination and Human Gate 3 recommendation

The reconciliation succeeds at conceptual alignment, repository/application boundaries, method-versus-product separation, and preservation of unresolved architecture choices. It does not yet meet the acceptance bar because two material contradictions remain in surfaces that can govern or persuade: the Lab replay's visitor-facing verification claims and the stale agent execution handoff.

**Recommendation: do not accept Human Gate 3 yet. Return those failures to a bounded Step 2 correction and repeat the affected Step 3 checks.** The correction should preserve the historical record and must not silently choose the replay-provenance path, editorial publication, runtime architecture, or demo scope. The draft site terminology and Git-ref freshness issue can be dispositioned in the same review, but they do not independently establish a hosted/publication claim or authorize an application change.
